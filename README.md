# USCode2 ✌️

## Overview

Welcome to **USCode2** - a collaborative coding interview preparation repository designed to help our team master technical interviews for U.S. tech companies. This repository serves as our central hub for sharing solutions, discussing problem-solving strategies, and tracking our progress through coding challenges.

Our mission is to build a strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, and system design while fostering a supportive learning environment where we can grow together and achieve our career goals in the U.S. tech industry.

## 💻 Workflow & Rules

- Use the format: `problem number_person name.py`
- Push your solutions before the study session day and merge during the study session time.
- Explain your solution process through comments (in English).
- Include LeetCode's suggested time complexity and space complexity as comments at the top of each file.

## Weekly Study Format

- **When:** Every Wednesday, 4:30 PM–6:00 PM
- **Homework:** Three problems, completed before the meeting.
- **Live coding:** One problem, completed in person during the meeting.

For the live-coding session, one member acts as the interviewee and solves the problem live. The other member acts as the interviewer/observer, asking questions and evaluating the solution.

The live-coding interviewee prepares the three homework problems, while the interviewer/observer prepares all four problems, including the live-coding problem.
